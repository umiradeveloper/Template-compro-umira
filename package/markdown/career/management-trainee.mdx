PT. Umira Sinergi Global, a rapidly growing national company in construction, engineering, and property, is launching its Management Trainee (MT) Program 2025 to develop future leaders in an Islamic, professional, and innovative work environment.

### Qualification

- Bachelor’s Degree (S1) in the following relevant fields:

- Civil Engineering

- Architecture

- Mechanical Engineering

- Electrical Engineering

- Minimum GPA: 3.30

- Fresh graduates are welcome to apply

- Maximum age of 25

- Proficient in Microsoft Office (Word, Excel, PowerPoint)

- Basic understanding of engineering design software such as AutoCAD or other relevant tools is an advantage

- Able to work quickly, accurately, and responsibly

- Strong communication, analytical, and problem-solving skills

- High integrity and commitment to a professional work system

- Willing to be placed in project locations (Yogyakarta, Surabaya, or Bali)

- Willing to commit to a service bond and undergo an intensive training program

### Benefit

- Intensive training and direct mentorship from senior leaders and top management

- Fast-track career opportunity to become a Supervisor or Division Head

- Structured development of both soft skills and hard skills

- Supportive, solid, and Islamic work environment that encourages professional growth
